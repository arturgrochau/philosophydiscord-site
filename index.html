The server

Not another shouting match

Most large politics Discords drown discussion in noise. Politics & Philosophy is built the other way round: a free-speech, European-leaning community where liberals, conservatives, socialists, traditionalists and people still making up their minds can explore their ideas, challenge each other and change their minds.

Argue about Kant. Argue about whether a particular war was just, or where an ideology actually came from. Or ask a plain question about the book you are stuck on, which is how a good half of these conversations start. Invite: discord.gg/RZgT4HBjCR

Why members stay

Built to be worth coming back to

  • Moderated on conduct, not opinion. No spam and no raids. Swap the argument for the insult and a moderator will pull you up on it. What you actually conclude is your own business.
  • Something happening most nights. Voice runs most evenings and the chat rarely goes quiet. The news desk updates daily, and there is usually a book on the go.
  • Ranks are earned. What you contribute counts, and so does how long you have been contributing it, so nobody farms their way to the top in a weekend.
  • Small enough to be recognised. 1,244 members. You're not a username in a 50,000-member firehose.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Politics & Philosophy Discord server?

A free-speech Discord community of 1,200+ members for political philosophy, ethics, ideology, geopolitics and European politics, across a fast-moving text floor, live voice chats and a book club.

Is it free to join?

Yes, completely free. Click a join link, finish the short onboarding, and you are in.

Do I need to know philosophy?

No. Members range from curious newcomers to the very well read. Good faith matters more than expertise.

What can I discuss?

Political philosophy, ethics, ideology, Marxism, liberalism and conservatism, geopolitics, current events, European politics, history and religion.

Can I make my own voice room?

Yes. Any member can open a voice room and run it: rename it, cap its size, lock it, mute, kick or ban, and hand it to someone else. There are fifteen commands, and none requires a moderator.

How active are the voice chats?

Voice runs most nights, on ethics, ideology and current events, and the conversation carries into text afterwards. The live count is shown above.

How do the ranks work?

You climb by taking part. Activity counts, and so does how long you have been around, so a rank reflects real participation rather than one busy weekend.

Is it a left-wing or right-wing server?

Neither by rule. It leans toward free speech and Europe and hosts the full spectrum. The point is to think positions through in good faith, not to enforce any.
